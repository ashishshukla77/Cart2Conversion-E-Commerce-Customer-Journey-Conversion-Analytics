# Business Recommendations Template

Run the notebooks on the public Olist dataset before filling numerical values.

## 1. Executive Findings
- Identify the top revenue-generating customer segments.
- Quantify repeat-purchase behavior.
- Identify cohorts with stronger/weaker retention.
- Identify high-value customers showing declining activity.

## 2. Retention Prioritization
### High-value / at-risk
Prioritize personalized retention offers and proactive communication.

### High-value / active
Use loyalty, cross-sell and premium-service strategies.

### New customers
Optimize onboarding and second-purchase conversion.

### Low-value / inactive
Use low-cost automated reactivation rather than expensive incentives.

## 3. Decision Framework
Rank customer segments by:
1. Revenue contribution
2. Customer count
3. Retention opportunity
4. Estimated campaign cost
5. Expected incremental value

Recommended pages:
